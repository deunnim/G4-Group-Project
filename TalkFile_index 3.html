♻️ Starting a Recycling Project

Learn six useful expressions and practice them in different ways.

1. Read the Story

A group of students wanted to find out how much waste their school produced each day. They decided to set up recycling boxes for paper and plastic. They also asked classmates to sort out their waste carefully. At first, many students did not pay attention to the recycling signs, so the team came up with a simple points game. With more students joining the project, the team was able to carry out the plan for an entire month.

2. Learn the Expressions

find out
알아내다, 알게 되다
to discover or learn something
EXAMPLE
We wanted to find out how much waste the school produced.
→ 우리는 학교에서 얼마나 많은 쓰레기가 나오는지 알아내고 싶었다.
set up
설치하다, 마련하다
to arrange or establish something
EXAMPLE
They set up recycling boxes near the classrooms.
→ 그들은 교실 근처에 재활용 상자를 설치했다.
sort out
분류하다, 정리하다
to separate things into groups
EXAMPLE
Please sort out the paper and plastic.
→ 종이와 플라스틱을 분류해 주세요.
pay attention to
~에 주의를 기울이다
to watch or listen carefully
EXAMPLE
Please pay attention to the recycling signs.
→ 재활용 표지판에 주의를 기울여 주세요.
come up with
~을 생각해 내다
to think of an idea or plan
EXAMPLE
The team came up with a simple points game.
→ 그 팀은 간단한 점수 게임을 생각해 냈다.
carry out
실행하다, 실시하다
to do or complete a plan
EXAMPLE
They carried out the plan for a month.
→ 그들은 한 달 동안 그 계획을 실행했다.
