Quick Review

Look at the six expressions before you start.

keep track of
계속 기록하다 / 확인하다
I keep track of my study time.
take a break
잠시 쉬다
Let's take a break.
stay away from
~을 멀리하다
I stay away from my phone at night.
get enough sleep
충분히 자다
I need to get enough sleep.
deal with
~에 대처하다 / 다루다
I can deal with stress.
stick to
~을 지키다 / 고수하다
I stick to my study plan.
